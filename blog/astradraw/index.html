MAKING · PROJECT NOTE

Research figures you can keep editing

A research figure rarely ends with its first draft. Labels move, arrows change, and the layout needs to work at paper size. Keeping these parts editable makes room for the next revision.

AstraDraw brings a paper, reference slides, and visual assets into a workflow for Astra in Codex. The paper explains the method; references communicate choices such as layout, color, and legends. The public repository provides examples and materials to start from.

Once the visual direction is settled, the workflow produces an editable PPT and a preview for checking the scientific relationships and presentation. For small changes, the aim is to revise the relevant objects while preserving the parts that already work.

The editable PPT and the preview serve different purposes: one keeps the parts available for revision; the other helps check the figure as a whole. Readable labels, clear relationships, and a balanced layout still need attention after the first draft.
