LMM-Track4D: keeping track of a moving world

What happens when a video question becomes a conversation? Keeping track of the same person takes geometry, motion, and a little memory.

Imagine watching a basketball clip. You ask which player took the shot, then follow up: “Where did he move next?” That tiny word, “he,” asks quite a lot of a model. It needs to carry a person, a place, and a moment from one answer into the next.

POSReasoner: when an object comes back

Keeping track of an object means deciding which observations to trust—and when to leave its memory alone.

Picture a sheep walking behind another sheep. For a moment, the one you were following disappears. Then a white, woolly shape comes back into view. Is that your sheep? A video segmentation model needs to answer this question while also drawing its outline. POSReasoner looks at what happens between those two jobs: deciding whether a new observation should change what the model remembers.

AstraDraw: from references to editable figures

A workflow for drawing research figures with Astra and refining them in PowerPoint.

A research figure rarely ends with its first draft. Labels move, arrows change, and the layout needs to work at paper size. Keeping these parts editable makes room for the next revision.
